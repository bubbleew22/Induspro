Pilot Operated Valves — Field Notes

• Internal pilot: pilot oil main P line se (X plug) · External pilot: alag line se
• Min pilot pressure typically 25–35 bar (spring force beat karna hota hai)
• PO check valve: pilot ratio 3:1 typical — 300 bar load → ~100 bar pilot chahiye
• Pilot valve chhota hota hai, main spool pressure + pilot se shift hota hai
• Vent-type PRV: remote vent port se pressure adjust / pump unload
• Dead-head (closed center): pump ka sara oil relief se jata hai → oil garam → cooler zaroori
